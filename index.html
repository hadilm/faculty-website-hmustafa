Curriculum Vitae

Hadil Mustafa

Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering whose work spans embedded systems, edge and IoT systems, FPGA-based computing, computer architecture, and engineering education.

Research

My research interests center on the design and implementation of embedded and computing systems, with current emphasis on artificial intelligence at the embedded edge, Internet of Things architectures, and resource-aware system design. My work also includes FPGA-based real-time simulation and engineering education.

Selected Funded Research

High-Speed, Cost-Effective Simulation and Design Techniques for PEPDS

Office of Naval Research (ONR) · Co-Investigator · 2018–2024 · $1.15M project

Research on FPGA-based real-time simulation, high-level synthesis, and low-latency communication for electric power-system applications. The work included FPGA communication protocols and simulation techniques supporting electric ship power-system research.

Embedded Artificial Intelligence

Chico State STEM Connection Collaborative (CSC²) · Summer 2026

Undergraduate research on the design, optimization, and deployment of artificial intelligence on embedded systems, extending ongoing interests in edge computing and resource-constrained intelligent systems.

IoT Systems and Engineering Education

Undergraduate research · 2023–2025

Research using Internet of Things system design and project-based learning to develop problem-solving, critical-thinking, and entrepreneurial-mindset skills in engineering students.
